ERROR 002

Maximum Refresh Rate Not Achieved

Issue
The monitor is operating below its maximum supported refresh rate.
Possible Cause
Display Stream Compression (DSC) is disabled, preventing the system from achieving the maximum supported resolution and refresh rate combination within the available display interface bandwidth.
Recommended Action
Enable DSC in the monitor's settings, if supported, to optimize display bandwidth utilization and enable the maximum supported refresh rate. Ensure that the graphics card, video interface, cable, and connected device support the required resolution, refresh rate, and DSC functionality.
