ERROR 002

Maximum Refresh Rate or Resolution Not Achieved

Issue
The monitor cannot run at its target specification of 3440 × 1440 @ 120Hz.
Possible Cause
Multiple factors may restrict the display timing: DSC disabled, insufficient bandwidth of video interface, incompatible video cable, graphics card limitation, outdated GPU driver, incorrect Windows display configuration, or source device not supporting 3440 × 1440 @ 120Hz with DSC.
Recommended Action
Check the monitor OSD DSC setting, video cable, display port/HDMI interface capability, graphics card specification and graphics driver version. If all hardware and OSD settings are confirmed ready, you may refer to the steps below to configure resolution and refresh rate.
⚙️ How to set Resolution & Refresh Rate (Windows 11)
1
Open Display Settings
Right-click blank area on desktop, then choose Display settings.
2
Select Native Resolution
In Display settings, find Scale and Display resolution. Choose 3440 × 1440 (Recommended) from the dropdown.
3
Open Advanced display
Scroll down the page and select Advanced display.
4
Set Refresh Rate to 120Hz
Select your monitor, then open Choose a refresh rate dropdown and pick 120 Hz.
Note: If 3440 × 1440 @ 120Hz is missing, double-check DSC toggle in monitor OSD, cable, port bandwidth and GPU compatibility.
