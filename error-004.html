ERROR 004

USB-C No Signal — USB 2.0-Only Cable Detected

Issue
No video signal is detected over the USB-C connection. The monitor identifies the attached cable as USB 2.0-only.
Possible Cause
The connected USB-C cable is a USB 2.0-only cable and does not support DisplayPort Alt Mode. It carries data and power but cannot transmit a valid video signal to the monitor.
Recommended Action
Replace the USB-C cable with one that supports video signal transmission (USB-C DP Alt Mode or Thunderbolt 3/4). Verify that the new cable is rated for the required resolution and refresh rate before connecting. Recommendation: Use a Lenovo official USB-C cable to ensure full compatibility and reliable video signal transmission.
